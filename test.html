Exercise 02

The case test

Seven patients — dogs, a cat and a horse. For each one: pick the technique you would use, then show where the stitches go. Sixty points for the decision, forty for the placement — and a reason for every case, right or wrong.

ⓘ How scoring works. 60 points for the best technique (32 for an acceptable alternative), 40 points for stitches placed within the marked zone. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere; reload to start again.
